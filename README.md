# Netflix Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze customer churn and subscription behavior through an interactive dashboard inspired by Netflix.

The dashboard explores customer churn rates, subscription types, payment methods, favorite genres, regional distribution, and customer retention.

The objective is to understand customer churn patterns and identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

## Business Objectives

- Measure customer churn and retention rates.
- Compare churn across subscription types.
- Analyze churn by payment method.
- Investigate churn patterns across customer preferences.
- Explore customer distribution across regions.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer loyalty.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | Total number of customers in the dataset |
| Churned Customers | Number of customers who have churned |
| Churn Rate | Percentage of customers who have churned |
| Retention Rate | Percentage of customers who have not churned |
| Monthly Revenue | Revenue recorded for the reporting period |

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Customer Churn Overview
Displays key customer metrics, including total customers, churned customers, churn rate, and retention rate.

### 2. Subscription Analysis
Compares customer counts and churn rates across Basic, Standard, and Premium subscription types.

### 3. Payment Method Analysis
Visualizes churn rates across different payment methods to identify potential patterns.

### 4. Customer Preference Analysis
Compares churn rates across favorite genres, including Sci-Fi, Romance, Action, Comedy, Thriller, Drama, and Documentary.

### 5. Regional Analysis
Provides a geographic view of customer churn and customer distribution across regions.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Allows users to explore the data using filters such as subscription type, region, churn status, and primary device, where available.

## Key Business Questions

- How many customers have churned?
- What percentage of customers have churned?
- Which subscription types have higher churn rates?
- How does churn vary across payment methods?
- Are churn rates different across customer genre preferences?
- How can Netflix improve customer retention?

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate subscription plans with higher churn rates.
- Analyze payment-related customer experience issues.
- Study customer preferences to identify opportunities for more relevant content recommendations.
- Compare churn patterns across regions and devices.
- Monitor retention and churn metrics regularly to evaluate improvement initiatives.
